Graveyard Keeper 2 gives almost no signposting about its story structure, and as of the v1.0 launch there is no official act-by-act breakdown from Lazy Bear Games or tinyBuild. This page reconstructs the opening act from a launch-week community 100% playthrough, as a progression-ordered sequence rather than a tips list. For first-days basics like storage, mastery, and the weekly schedule, start with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).

## What Counts as Act 1 in Graveyard Keeper 2?

With no official act structure published, "Act 1" here means the opening stretch before the town's reclamation is fully under way. In one launch-week playthrough the major unlocks arrived in the order below. Treat it as a spine, not a checklist — the game rarely ties these to a fixed clock, so your order may vary.

## The Very First Unlocks: A Quest Reward and the Tech Trees

The first thing the player noted was a quest reward that can later be used to write prayers and books. Using it earned their first achievement, reported as "beginning the journey." At the same moment they opened the technology screen and headed out to greet the corpse-bearing donkey that opens the corpse loop. Both tracks are live this early, and the technology side is broken down in the [tech points and mastery guide](/guides/tech-points-mastery/).

## Getting Jack to the City and Into the Church

Early progression hinges on a character called Jack. Sending Jack to the city via his dodgy boat earned another achievement — and brought back a nun, the key that finally let the player go inside the church, which had been visible but closed until then. If you are stuck outside the church in the opening hours, this is the thread to pull: the interior unlocks through the Jack-and-nun sequence, not by exploring.

## Ceremonies, Faith, and the Offering

Once inside, a nun performs a ceremony. Ceremonies are the source of faith, and performing your first one earned an achievement reported as the first-ceremony milestone. From there the player began collecting money from the offering — the faith-and-sermon loop that funds a lot of later progression. The [day-of-week calendar](/guides/day-of-week-calendar/) decides when these beats are available.

## The First Town Order

The first town order was supplying Jack with iron. Completing it awarded what the player reported as a copper award plus 20 gratitude, along with the achievement for a first completed town order. Town orders become a recurring income and reputation loop rather than a one-off, and the [town rebuilding guide](/guides/town-rebuilding/) follows that chain past this first delivery.

## Smelting, Building Kits, and the Industry Chain

Mid-early industry followed a clear material chain, worth understanding as a pipeline rather than separate chores.

| Step | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smelting | ore | ingots |
| Stone kits | stone + polished stone | stone kits |
| Building kits | stone kits + bricks | building kits |
| Rebuilding | planks + oiled planks | town structures |

The player earned an achievement at 100 ingots smelted, a useful marker that you are through the early industry hump. Planks and oiled planks feed directly into rebuilding the town.

## Cooking and the 100-Dish Counter

Cooking reached 100 dishes, another cumulative-counter achievement that advances on its own if you keep a routine food loop alive — which doubles as the thing that keeps your keeper fed and your energy up. The [food and energy page](/items/food-and-energy/) covers the self-care side.

## Meeting the Bandits

Partway through, the player met the bandits — reported as a character named Hans alongside a skeletal companion. It is a story beat rather than a system gate, but it marks a plot shift and a useful checkpoint on the way to reclamation.

## Town Reclamation Begins

Reclamation is the centerpiece of the mid-act and runs on several connected systems:

- Central command, where the effort is organized.
- Recruiting battle squads to put bodies on the field.
- Building fortifications and barricades to defend reclaimed ground.
- Barracks slots, which cap your fielded squads.
- The first battle against the zombies, which in the player's words marks the beginning of the town's reclamation.

After that first battle the player placed crafted barricades, captured flags, removed zombie spawn points, and earned an emblem that unlocked an additional squad slot. The [tower defense guide](/guides/tower-defense/) covers the battle systems in depth.

## Alchemy and the Blue Tech Points

Alchemy came online later in the act. Making potions earned an achievement at 25 potions, and the alchemy and zombie-juice line was where the player reported earning blue tech points specifically. If you are hunting a particular tech-point colour, this thread is one documented source of it.

## Social Threads: Bringing NPCs Together

Two side threads each earned an achievement: bringing Linda and Albert together, and bringing Herbert and Agatha together. They are easy to miss because they are conversation-driven rather than quest-log-driven, so note them on a first pass.

## Market Stalls and Attracting Townspeople

Later still, the player built market stalls and began attracting townspeople back. The [achievements list](/guides/achievements/) tracks the related milestone of building all the market stalls.

## How Do You Know Act 1 Is Ending?

There is no official trigger for the end of Act 1 as of the v1.0 launch. The clearest signal comes from pre-launch demo footage rather than the release build. A demo stream recorded 2026-09-18 contained an explicit "Trigger the End of Act 1" moment near the end of that stream's content, immediately followed by a "So What's Next?" segment; the demo also included three "back story cut scene" segments and a dedicated "Battle Mechanic" segment.

Treat that boundary carefully: the demo was a slice, and its act structure may not match the launch version's. The demo's saved progress did carry over to the full game at launch, according to pre-launch coverage, but carry-over says nothing about how the acts are divided. The honest answer is that the opening act ends somewhere after the reclamation arc is under way and the market stalls go up, and the "So What's Next?" beat is the shape to watch for, not a to-the-minute trigger.

## What Should You Do After the Opening Act?

Keep the four loops this act establishes running: the faith-and-offering loop from the church, the smelting and building-kit chain, the reclamation defense, and the easy-to-drop NPC side threads. Read [what is new versus the first game](/guides/whats-new-vs-graveyard-keeper-1/) if you are arriving from the original, and keep the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) open for the fundamentals.

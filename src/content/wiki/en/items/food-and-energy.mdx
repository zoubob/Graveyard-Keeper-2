## How Does Energy Work in Graveyard Keeper 2?

Every action in Graveyard Keeper 2 draws on a stamina bar — digging, chopping, hammering, crafting, hauling. Community beginner coverage calls energy "the resource that controls everything," and the most frustrating way to lose an in-game afternoon is running it dry halfway through a job. Food is the only refill that scales, so the way you eat is really the way you pace your day.

For context: Lazy Bear Games and tinyBuild released Graveyard Keeper 2 on 2026-09-22, at $24.99 on Steam (App ID 4358690). The stamina economy below is community-reported from launch-window coverage rather than an official stat sheet, so treat specific figures as leads rather than documentation.

A quick map of the food groups people actually rely on:

| Food source | Where it comes from | How it holds up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Berries and mushrooms | Foraged along your early routes | Survival only; minimal energy |
| Bread | Wheat to mill to dough to oven | The early-game energy backbone |
| Vegetable salad | Garden carrots, beets and onions | Buff before precise surgery work |
| Camp meals | Cooked for night sentinels | Reported route to advanced recipes |

## Why Is Bread the First Real Energy Answer?

Community beginner tips are blunt about this: berries and mushrooms should not be a plan. They return what one piece of coverage calls "a minimal, almost symbolic amount of energy compared to the real cost of digging a grave or performing an autopsy." They get you through the first few minutes, then stop making sense the moment your workload grows.

The wheat chain is the fix. The moment the farmstead unlocks, coverage recommends treating the wheat, mill and bread structure as a top priority:

1. Plant and harvest wheat at the farmstead as soon as it opens.
2. Rush to the mill, which turns wheat into flour "at a very generous ratio."
3. Carry the flour to the kitchen and make dough.
4. Bake the dough in the oven for bread.

Bread restores far more energy than anything you can pick up off the ground in your first few hours, and the loop is repeatable. If energy problems keep coming back, the answer is almost always that the bread line is not running consistently. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) walks through the first-days order that gets this chain online, and the [money-making guide](/guides/money-making-guide/) explains why a working kitchen pays for itself in saved days.

## When Should You Eat a Vegetable Salad?

The most-cited food trick is surgical. Community advanced-tips coverage reports the habit directly: "I always consume a vegetable salad before any delicate procedure to guarantee flawless surgeries on the operating table."

The reported recipe is two carrots, two beets and two onions harvested from your own garden plot. The effect the same coverage describes:

- You receive three surgical mastery points that last a full 7 and 1/2 minutes.
- The bonus scales with your farming mastery and the quality of the dish, and can reportedly yield up to four extra anatomy points.
- That window is enough to open the morgue door and clean several bodies back to back without rushing.
- Players report it "practically eliminates all chances of making that terrible surgeon's mistake when extracting vital organs."

This is single-source community reporting, so treat the exact point values as unconfirmed. The behavioural advice is the durable part: keep those vegetables in continuous rotation so you never open an operation empty-handed. Gardening throughput is what makes or breaks the habit, which is why the [gardening and perks guide](/zombies/gardening-and-perks/) is worth reading alongside this page, and the [organs guide](/items/organs-guide/) covers what you are actually protecting with those careful surgeries.

## What Is the Night Camp and Why Does It Matter?

Community advanced coverage describes a night camp southwest of the "two brothers area." Sentinels or guards appear there only at night, and they accept prepared meals carried in your backpack. Bringing them food regularly reportedly unlocks new dialogues and interactions that are not marked on the regular map.

The attraction, per the same coverage, is exclusive cooking recipes geared toward advanced bonuses. Players report stockpiling these special dishes before long stretches of heavy carpentry or mining, using them to secure large energy reserves. One honest caveat: the exact return is reported to vary according to the items you deliver, and this is again single-source community material, so treat it as a lead to test rather than a documented system.

## Does Sleeping Restore Energy in Graveyard Keeper 2?

Sleeping recovers energy, but it does something else at the same time, and the two effects do not move together. Community coverage notes that sleeping without being tired does recover energy but barely reduces insanity. When your actual goal is lowering insanity, the reported advice is to wait until the character is tired or exhausted before you sleep, so one night serves both purposes.

That makes sleep a resource with two outcomes rather than one, and it is why a stocked kitchen matters beyond convenience: if you can top up energy with food, you can save sleep for the nights when you actually need the insanity relief. The [insanity guide](/guides/insanity-guide/) covers the full mechanic; here the takeaway is simply that food and sleep are substitutes, and food is the one you can control.

## What Food Plan Should a New Player Follow?

A short sequence that follows the community coverage:

1. Forage berries and mushrooms only long enough to reach the farmstead.
2. Set up wheat farming the moment the farm unlocks, and build the mill, kitchen and oven chain.
3. Run bread as your default energy source and keep the line stocked.
4. Plant carrots, beets and onions in continuous rotation for vegetable salads before surgery.
5. Deliver meals to the night camp once your kitchen is stable, and test the recipes you unlock.

Energy is the resource every other plan spends, so it is worth solving early and solving properly. Once it stops interrupting you, the [items overview](/items/getting-started/) is the place to decide what to build next.
